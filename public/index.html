Performer, technician, designer, o someone with a specialized skill. Start with what you need done.

“Hindi lumalamig ang aircon namin.”

“May kilala kang clown para sa birthday?”

“Kailangan ko ng technician sa lumang camera.”

You don’t need to know the job title.
